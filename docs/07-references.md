# 07 — Reference (primární zdroje)

## Nanotechnologie & dodávání

  patterns. *Nature* 440:297–302. — DNA origami koncept.
- **Douglas S.M., Bachelet I., Church G.M.** (2012). A logic-gated nanorobot
  for targeted transport of molecular payloads. *Science* 335:831–834.

  14:1084–1087. — LNP přehled.

  Toll-like receptors: the impact of nucleoside modification. *Immunity*
  23:165–175. — pseudouridinová mRNA.
- **Cheng Q. et al.** (2020). Selective organ targeting (SORT) nanoparticles.
  *Nat Nanotechnol* 15:313–320. — extrahepatální LNP cílení.
- **Hou X. et al.** (2021). Lipid nanoparticles for mRNA delivery. *Nat Rev
  Mater* 6:1078–1094.
- **Yang Q., Lai S.K.** (2015). Anti-PEG immunity. *WIREs Nanomed Nanobiotechnol*
  7:655–677.
